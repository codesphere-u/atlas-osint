# Code of Conduct

ATLAS OSINT adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment: empathy and kindness, respect for differing
opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on
what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes: sexualised language or imagery; trolling, insulting or derogatory comments;
public or private harassment; **publishing others' private information (doxxing)**; and other conduct which could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Project-specific rule: no misuse

Because this is an intelligence tool, contributions, issues and discussions must not:

- request or provide help to stalk, harass, dox, track or surveil a person;
- add capabilities that bypass the platform's authorisation, consent, scope or privacy controls;
- add sources that harvest private data, breached data, credentials, or that require unauthorised access;
- include real personal data in fixtures, screenshots or bug reports (use synthetic data).

Such contributions will be closed and may lead to a ban.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers (see `SECURITY.md` for the contact channel). All reports are reviewed
promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate, following the Contributor Covenant enforcement
guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
